# 4) Decisioning and risk segmentation

## Risk tiers (example)
- Excellent: PD < 2%
- Good: 2–5%
- Fair: 5–10%
- Poor: 10–20%
- Very High: ≥ 20%

## Strategy levers per tier
- Approval threshold
- Pricing floors/spreads
- Line/amount caps
- Verification requirements (policy)

## What a Senior Business Analyst should deliver
- A recommended threshold with justification (cost/benefit)
- Tier-level KPIs: approval rate, bad rate, expected loss, profit per booked loan
- A rollout plan (champion/challenger, monitoring cadence, guardrails)
