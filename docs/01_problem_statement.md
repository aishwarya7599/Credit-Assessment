# 1) Business problem (Capital One–style)

## Objective
Build a model that estimates **Probability of Default (PD)** at origination and operationalize it into:
1. **Approval decisioning** (probability thresholding)
2. **Risk segmentation** (tiers for strategy)
3. **Indicative pricing guidance** (risk-based rate floors/spreads)

## Why it matters
In consumer lending, value comes from optimizing the trade-off between:
- **Credit losses** (net charge-offs)
- **Growth** (approval rate)
- **Unit economics** (risk-adjusted margin)

A Senior Business Analyst is expected to convert analytics into decisions, quantify trade-offs, and recommend a rollout and monitoring plan.
